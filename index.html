About me

After several tragically failed attempts to describe myself, I decided to impose some constraints to see if it works. I limited myself to only using 5 statements to depict my personality (kinda corny but I just can't escape it):

  • Constraints can be freeing and freedom can be constraining
  • Generally, I find beauty in simplicity, dissonance and abstraction
